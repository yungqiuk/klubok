Support

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Questions, ideas or a problem with the game? Write to yungqifr@gmail.com — we answer every message.

How do I play?

Spin the ball with one finger, zoom and twist it with two. In Unravel, tap a tile and find all its copies; a wrong tile resets the choice. In Assemble, tap the place on the ball where the tile shown at the bottom belongs. Settings → How to Play shows the rules again.

I bought No Ads but see ads

Open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Reward videos stay available — they are only shown when you choose to watch one.

How do I change my ad consent?

Settings → Ad privacy settings (shown where the law requires it).
