Klubok: Gem Sphere Puzzle

Last updated: October 18, 2026

A calm puzzle you can turn in your hands — a ball made of picture tiles.

Unravel: find every copy of a tile on the 3D ball and clear it layer by layer down to the core. Assemble: put the tiles of a real picture back in place — the Earth and the Moon, old world maps, Van Gogh, Hokusai, Monet and nature panoramas.

Free to play. Coins earned in the game unlock patterns, backgrounds and pictures. The game shows ads; an optional one-time purchase, No Ads, removes ads between levels.
