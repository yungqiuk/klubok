Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Klubok has no accounts and does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information.

Your game progress

Levels, coins, settings and unlocked items are stored only on your device. We do not have a server and do not receive this data.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: an optional reward video (you choose to watch it) and an ad between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect device information such as the identifier for vendors, an approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions and diagnostic data. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy settings. Klubok does not request permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. How Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

The No Ads purchase is processed by Apple. We never see your payment details; Apple only tells the game that the purchase was made.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
